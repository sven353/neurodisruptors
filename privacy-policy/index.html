Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Sven Mulfinger operates neurodisruptors.com and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how your data is collected, used and safeguarded when you visit this website or send an inquiry.

Data controller

Sven Mulfinger, NIF ES4339734W, Impact Hub Barcelona, Carrer de Pujades 94-96, 08005 Barcelona, Spain. Email: info@stralea.com.

Data we collect and why

We collect the personal information you choose to give us through the inquiry form: your name, email address, the type of engagement you are interested in and, if you provide them, your company, role and the context of your inquiry.

We use this information only to reply to your inquiry and, where you ask for it, to arrange a conversation or send a proposal. The legal basis is your consent and the steps needed before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

Processors

Form submissions are received and stored on our behalf by our hosting provider, Netlify, which may process data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards required by the GDPR. If you contact Sven through LinkedIn, LinkedIn's own privacy policy applies. We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information.

Retention

We keep inquiry data for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any resulting engagement, and delete it when it is no longer necessary, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and fonts are served from our own domain. External services you open from this site, such as LinkedIn, apply their own cookie policies.

Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. You may withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, write to info@stralea.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es).

Data security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new date.
