Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Stralea, located at Impact Hub Barcelona, Carrer de Pujades 94-96, 08005 Barcelona, operates www.neurodisruptors.com and is committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard your data when you visit this website.

Data controller

Stralea, Impact Hub Barcelona, Carrer de Pujades 94-96, 08005 Barcelona, Spain. Email: info@stralea.com.

Data we collect and why

We collect the personal information you choose to give us when you send a request through the contact form or write to us by email: your name, email address and, if you provide them, your organisation, role and the context of your request.

We use this information only to reply to your request and, where you ask for it, to arrange a conversation or send a proposal. The legal basis is your consent and the steps needed before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

Processors

Form submissions are received and stored by our hosting provider on our behalf. If you choose to book a call, the booking is handled by Calendly under its own privacy policy. We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information.

Retention

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting engagement, and delete it when it is no longer necessary, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and fonts are served from our own domain. External services you open from this site, such as LinkedIn or Calendly, apply their own cookie policies.

Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. You may withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, write to info@stralea.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es).

Data security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new date.
