Cognitive DimensionNatural Spike (High Leverage)Operating Valley (Systemic Friction)Executive Scaffolding Required
Systemic Synthesis Identifies non-linear market shifts years ahead. Translating intuition into step-by-step documentation feels painfully slow. Visual roadmaps and dedicated operational integrators.
Crisis Hyperfocus Exceptional clarity and decisive execution in turnaround or high-stakes ambiguity. Low-stimulation routine reporting triggers acute cognitive drag. Elimination of context-switching and full administrative delegation.
Relational Intuition Senses unspoken boardroom tensions and culture drift immediately. Absorbs emotional turbulence, risking boundary collapse and burnout. Strict energy recovery protocols and formalised governance boundaries.

The Spiky Profile is an operational map of executive cognitive load. It does not assess personality or diagnose any condition. It shows where executive leverage peaks under pressure, where cognitive fatigue accumulates and what structural scaffolding is needed to lead sustainably.

  3. 03 / Scaffold

Strategic Context

Frequently asked questions.

Key distinctions drawn from executive sparring and Dr. Sven Mulfinger's book, The Non-Linear Operating System.

What is a Spiky Profile in executive leadership?

A Spiky Profile is an operational framework developed by Dr. Sven Mulfinger and research collaborators that maps cognitive variance across high-leverage innovation spikes and operational execution valleys.

Rather than demanding flat competency across every domain, it designs organisational scaffolding around a leader's natural cognitive architecture.

How does private strategic sparring differ from conventional executive coaching?

Conventional coaching focuses on behavioural remediation and rounding out perceived deficits, whereas strategic sparring is an intellectual partnership designed for founders and CEOs whose thinking outpaces standard operating cadences.

We focus on cognitive load management, high-stakes decision architecture and governance alignment without therapeutic pretence.

Why do non-linear leaders struggle with traditional corporate time management?

Time management models fail non-linear thinkers because their true cognitive constraint is energy rather than time.

Neural throughput requires the alignment of motivation (ignition), activation (intensity) and physiological capacity; attempting to force deep strategic output during low-activation calendar blocks causes compounding exhaustion.

Is the Spiky Profile a clinical or psychiatric diagnostic tool?

No: the Spiky Profile is exclusively a leadership and management framework designed to map executive leverage and workplace friction.

It does not provide medical, psychological or psychiatric diagnosis for ADHD, autism or other neurodevelopmental conditions.

What formats are available for enterprise and board engagements?

Enterprise engagements are structured into two principal formats: a 45-minute keynote address (Asymmetric Minds in the C-Suite) and half-day alignment offsites (Bridging the Founder-Board Interface) designed for boards, venture partners and executive committees.

An enterprise workshop, Designing Systems for Cognitive Spikes, is also available for executive teams and HR leaders.

Where is the research behind The Non-Linear Operating System conducted?

The framework draws on ongoing academic research collaborations with faculty at IESE Business School and IE Business School.

This research is combined with field analysis across more than 50 serial entrepreneurs and CEOs with a non-linear mind.
