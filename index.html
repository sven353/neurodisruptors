03 · The Spiky Profile Framework

A working map of cognitive range, built for decisions.

Retire diagnostic labels. Replace them with an operating map that locates breakthrough capacity, names the valleys and specifies the scaffolding each one needs.

A spiky profile, whatever you call it.

Some leaders arrive with a label: ADHD, autism, giftedness, dyslexia, dyspraxia or dyscalculia. Others never had a diagnosis and simply know their mind runs differently from the people around them. Many recognise themselves in more than one.

Neurodivergence is the umbrella term. We speak of a spiky profile or a non-linear mind, because in leadership what matters is how your mind works: where it is exceptional, where it costs you energy and what helps it run well.

A label can explain the past. For the next quarter, the useful question is what your good days and your bad days look like and what separates them.

Gifts and costs, profile by profile

Every strong point comes with a weak spot. Pick a profile to see how that tends to look at work and what helps.

ADHD: the gift, the cost and what helps
PatternThe giftThe costWhat helps
Sees round corners Spots where the market is going long before the numbers show it. Explaining the idea step by step, in writing, feels slow and painful. A visual roadmap and a strong number two who turns ideas into plans.
Calm in a crisis Gets sharp and decisive when things go wrong and the stakes are high. Routine reporting and admin drain energy fast. Hand off the routine, protect deep-work blocks and cut down on switching between tasks.
Reads the room Picks up tension in a meeting before anyone says a word. Takes on other people's stress and finds it hard to switch off. Real recovery time, clear boundaries and someone to think out loud with.
Dyslexia: the gift, the cost and what helps
PatternThe giftThe costWhat helps
Big-picture thinker Grasps the core of a problem quickly and sees how the pieces connect. Turning that clarity into emails, reports and documents takes far more effort than the thinking itself. Talk it through first, then let someone else or a tool draft it.
Strong in the room Persuasive and quick in conversation. Ideas land when they are spoken. Written work can look weaker than the person behind it. Judge ideas on content rather than spelling. Voice notes, sketches and short calls help too.
Pattern spotter Notices contradictions and connections others miss. Dense documents and long reading under time pressure are tiring. One-page summaries, diagrams and time to prepare before key meetings.
Giftedness: the gift, the cost and what helps
PatternThe giftThe costWhat helps
High resolution Sees details, risks and knock-on effects early. Shallow or repetitive work is draining, not just boring. Real complexity to work on and less low-value noise.
Connects everything Links ideas across fields and holds the whole system in mind at once. The mind keeps running long after the problem is solved. Long blocks of thinking time, followed by proper breaks.
Strong sense of meaning Cares deeply about doing the right thing well. Inconsistency and poor decisions are hard to let go of and can read as impatience. A partner who enjoys structure and room to say what does not add up.
Autism: the gift, the cost and what helps
PatternThe giftThe costWhat helps
Deep focus Can go further into a hard problem than almost anyone. Interruptions and constant context switching are very costly. Protected focus time and fewer, better-run meetings.
Straight talk Honest, precise and consistent, with no hidden agenda. Unwritten rules and office politics take a lot of energy to decode. Clear expectations, explicit decisions and agendas shared in advance.
Sees the system Spots flaws in processes, logic and data that others accept. Last-minute changes and noisy settings are exhausting. Notice of changes, a quiet place to work and written follow-ups.
Dyspraxia: the gift, the cost and what helps
PatternThe giftThe costWhat helps
Strategic overview Sees the overall plan and where it needs to end up. Sequencing the many small practical steps to get there is tiring. Checklists, templates and an operations lead who owns the logistics.
Finds another way Used to working round obstacles, so comes up with unusual solutions. Tasks others do on autopilot, like travel, set-up and paperwork, take real effort. Automate or delegate the routine and build buffer time into travel days.
Good with people Articulate and empathic, good at explaining and bringing people with them. Busy, fast-moving settings and writing by hand in a live session are hard work. Typed notes, digital tools, quieter rooms and fewer room changes in a day.
Twice-exceptional: the gift, the cost and what helps
PatternThe giftThe costWhat helps
Hidden range Works at a very high level in areas of strength. The strengths hide the struggle and the struggle hides the strengths, so both get misread. Name both openly and shape the role around the peaks.
Big leaps Makes jumps others cannot follow and cracks problems others gave up on. Simple tasks can trip them up, which is baffling and wears down confidence. Support for the basics without anyone questioning the ability.
High standards Raises the quality bar and spots what others miss. The gap between what they see and what they can deliver on a bad day is exhausting. Honest feedback, recovery time and a sparring partner who sees both sides.

Strategic Context

Frequently asked questions.

Key distinctions drawn from executive sparring and Dr. Sven Mulfinger's book, The Non-Linear Operating System.

What is a Spiky Profile in executive leadership?

A Spiky Profile is a leadership framework developed by Dr. Sven Mulfinger that maps where a leader's thinking creates exceptional leverage (spikes) and where it meets friction (valleys).

It covers ten operational dimensions, from systemic synthesis and crisis hyperfocus to routine cadence and calendar fragmentation. Neurodisruptors uses it to design the roles, rhythms and support around a leader instead of asking for an even profile across every domain.

Is this about ADHD?

Partly: ADHD is one of several ways a mind can run on a spiky profile, alongside autism, giftedness, dyslexia, dyspraxia and dyscalculia.

Many leaders recognise themselves in more than one. The work focuses on how your mind operates as a leader, with or without a diagnosis. It does not diagnose any condition; that stays with doctors and psychologists.

Who is Dr. Sven Mulfinger?

Dr. Sven Mulfinger is the founder of Neurodisruptors and a Barcelona-based executive coach, offsite facilitator and strategic advisor to founders and C-suite teams across Europe.

He trained as an engineer, holds a PhD in biotech engineering and is Lead Partner at Kuma Partners. He is writing The Non-Linear Operating System with research collaborators at IESE Business School and IE Business School. Neurodisruptors grew out of his own ADHD diagnosis at 50.

Do I need a diagnosis to work with Neurodisruptors?

No: the work starts from how your mind operates as a leader, so a formal diagnosis is neither required nor assessed.

Some clients have a diagnosis of ADHD, autism, dyslexia or another profile, others suspect one and many simply know they think differently. Questions about diagnosis or treatment belong with a doctor or psychologist.

Where can I get a formal diagnosis?

For a formal assessment of ADHD, autism, dyspraxia or giftedness as an adult, Neurodisruptors refers clients to Dr. Andreina Mendez, a medical doctor who sees adults in person in Madrid and London and remotely across Europe.

A diagnosis is not needed for the work, but it can help. Many leaders start asking once one of their children has been diagnosed. Understanding how your own mind operates can then be a relief for parents and children alike. Sometimes you simply want to know for sure, which a medical assessment can give you. You can contact Dr. Mendez through andreinamendez.com or LinkedIn and mention that Neurodisruptors referred you.

How does private strategic sparring differ from conventional executive coaching?

Conventional coaching often works on rounding out weaknesses, while strategic sparring with Neurodisruptors is a confidential thinking partnership for founders and CEOs whose minds move faster than standard operating rhythms.

Sessions focus on live decisions, cognitive load and the structures around the leader, such as roles, calendar design and board communication. It is executive work and involves no therapy.

How does private sparring work in practice?

Private sparring is a confidential, retainer-based partnership with Dr. Sven Mulfinger, held in person in Barcelona or by secure video, with direct access between sessions during critical decisions.

It starts with a confidential first conversation to check fit. Sessions run in English, Spanish, German or French. Meetings in other European cities are arranged when needed.

Is there an executive coach in Barcelona for founders with ADHD?

Yes: Neurodisruptors, led by Dr. Sven Mulfinger in Barcelona, offers confidential strategic sparring for founders and CEOs with ADHD, giftedness, dyslexia and other spiky profiles.

Work takes place in person in Barcelona, in other European cities including London, Berlin, Paris, Madrid and Zurich, or remotely, in four languages.

Why do non-linear leaders struggle with traditional corporate time management?

For many non-linear leaders the real constraint is energy rather than time, so a calendar of evenly spaced blocks works against how their minds perform.

Deep strategic work needs motivation, activation and physical capacity to line up. Sparring helps leaders plan around those peaks, protect them and move routine work elsewhere.

How can a board support a founder with ADHD?

A board supports a founder with ADHD best by reading uneven output as a pattern to design around, with clear roles, the right operating partner and reporting formats that fit how the founder thinks.

The half-day offsite Bridging the Founder-Board Interface works through this with boards, investors and executive committees and keeps the conversation on leadership and governance.

Is the Spiky Profile a clinical or psychiatric diagnostic tool?

No: the Spiky Profile is exclusively a leadership and management framework designed to map executive leverage and workplace friction.

It does not provide medical, psychological or psychiatric diagnosis for ADHD, autism or other neurodevelopmental conditions.

What formats are available for enterprise and board engagements?

Neurodisruptors offers two principal formats: a 45-minute keynote (Asymmetric Minds in the C-Suite) and half-day alignment offsites (Bridging the Founder-Board Interface) for boards, venture partners and executive committees.

An enterprise workshop, Designing Systems for Cognitive Spikes, is also available for executive teams and HR leaders.
