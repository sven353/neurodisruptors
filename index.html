01 · Executive Sparring

One-to-one sparring for the person who carries the final decision.

Most performance advice assumes steady output. Many founders with ADHD, giftedness or dyslexia, diagnosed or not, run on spikes: exceptional range in some areas and real friction in others. Sparring works on that pattern directly, at the level of the decisions you are facing now.

  1. 01

    Your Spiky Profile

    Where your capacity runs high, where effort quietly accumulates and where friction shows up when the pressure rises.

  2. 02

    Energy over the clock

    Structuring decisions, meetings and deep work around your energy cycles so the calendar stops working against the way you think.

  3. 03

    The cost of compensation

    Making visible the private systems you built to mask friction, and deciding which of them are still worth what they cost you.

  4. 04

    Leading other spiky minds

    Reading variability in your strongest people accurately, and designing their roles around what they do exceptionally well.

Confidential, in person in Barcelona or remote. Scope and cadence are agreed after a first conversation.

02 · Workshops & Keynotes

For boards, executive teams and the people who hire them.

Roles, evaluations and succession plans are usually designed around predictable execution. When non-linear leaders operate inside those systems, friction shows up as conflict, burnout or misread performance. These sessions make the underlying architecture visible to the people who decide.

  • Keynote

    ADHD, Giftedness and Dyslexia in the C-Suite

    Why traits read as inconsistency in top performers often describe a distinct cognitive architecture, and what changes once leaders can see it.

    Leadership forums, conferences, company events

  • Board workshop

    Why Boards Misread Non-Linear Leaders as Unreliable

    A working session on reading variability correctly in founders and senior executives, and on spotting burnout that comes from structure instead of personality.

    Boards, investors, executive committees

  • Team session

    Designing Teams Around Cognitive Spikes

    Practical work on structuring roles, feedback and expectations around how non-linear minds actually perform.

    HR, L&D and people managers

03 · The Spiky Profile Framework

A working map of cognitive range, built for decisions.

The framework replaces diagnostic labels with a map leaders can use. It shows where capacity peaks, where it dips and how that shape changes under load, and gives founders, boards and teams a shared language for it.

  1. Map

    Ten cognitive dimensions, one profile

    A short assessment and a structured conversation plot how you perceive, decide and execute across ten dimensions.

  2. Read

    How the shape behaves under load

    We look at what happens to the profile during a raise, a scale-up phase, a board conflict or a crisis.

  3. Redesign

    Structures that fit the profile

    Roles, decision rights, working rhythms and team composition are adjusted so the organisation draws on the peaks.

  4. Share

    A language the board can use

    The profile gives co-founders, boards and HR a precise way to discuss performance without falling back on labels.

04 · About

Dr. Sven Mulfinger

Executive coach, offsite facilitator and strategic advisor to founders and C-suite teams across Europe.

Sven trained as an engineer and holds a PhD in biotech engineering. He spent years building start-ups, including a venture in women's mental health, before moving into advisory work with founders and leadership teams. He is Lead Partner at Kuma Partners in Barcelona, where he prepares and facilitates leadership offsites for scale-ups.

Neurodisruptors grew out of a late ADHD diagnosis at 50 and a pattern he kept recognising in the founders he worked with. The research, the book and the Spiky Profile framework all come from that work.
