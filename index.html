Strategic Context

Frequently asked questions.

Direct answers for founders, boards and HR leaders weighing an engagement.

How does executive sparring differ from traditional executive coaching?

Most executive coaching works from a uniform competency model and focuses on rounding out weaknesses. Executive sparring with Dr. Sven Mulfinger is a confidential, high-velocity thought partnership on the decisions a founder or CEO is facing now. It maps the leader's spiky cognitive profile (ADHD, giftedness, rapid synthesis) and builds operational scaffolding around those strengths so they no longer need to be masked.

What is the Spiky Profile framework?

The Spiky Profile is an operational model developed by Dr. Sven Mulfinger, grounded in executive case studies and his research collaboration with IESE Business School. It maps a leader's cognitive capacity across perception, synthesis and execution, identifying breakthrough strengths alongside operational valleys. It replaces clinical labels with a non-clinical language that founders, boards and teams can use to align roles and governance.

What engagements are available for boards, investors and HR leaders?

Three formats: a keynote, The Non-Linear Edge in the C-Suite; a board working session, Bridging the Cognitive Interface, on reading executive variance and preventing founder burnout; and an enterprise workshop, Designing Systems for Cognitive Spikes, for executive teams and HR. Sessions run from 45 minutes to half a day and are scoped after a first conversation.

Where are engagements delivered and in which languages?

Neurodisruptors is based in Barcelona. Dr. Sven Mulfinger works in person in Barcelona, London, Berlin, Paris and Zurich, across other European business hubs and confidentially by video. Sparring, keynotes and board workshops are delivered in English, Spanish, German or French.
